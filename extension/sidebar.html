How it works

File names

  • Empty filename: named after the video title.
  • Clutter like (Official Video) or [HD] is removed.
  • Name it Artist - Song to set the artist and title your player shows.
  • Only a spaced dash splits: "Jay-Z" stays whole.

The MP3

  • Best quality, with the thumbnail as cover art.
  • Album, year and track number when the site has them.

Saving

  • Your folder is remembered, default Documents\YT-MP3-Downloads.
  • Existing files are never overwritten.

Shortcuts

  • Open the sidebar on a video: the link fills in.
  • Right-click a video link: "Download as MP3".
  • Playlists and albums: only the first track.

Updates

  • Automatic. A download after a YouTube change may take a bit longer.
